Canais Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

Canais Uploader is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Brittle Empires, Impérios em Queda, Print The Receipt, Akta Milczenia and Skin Crawl Stories to upload and schedule the owner's own videos, set thumbnails and add captions through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

Only the owner's own YouTube channels, with the owner's explicit OAuth consent. The tool uploads videos, thumbnails, captions and metadata that the owner created, and reads basic channel information to confirm the correct channel.

Data we store

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in the owner's private infrastructure (GitHub Secrets) and are never shared, sold or disclosed to third parties. No data from other YouTube users is collected.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
